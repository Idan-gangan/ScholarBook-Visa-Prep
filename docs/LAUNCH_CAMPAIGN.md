2. **Product walkthrough (Day 2–3):** Screen recording: Register → Verify email → My Profile → Test microphone → Mock Interview → Feedback report. Blur names, emails, student answers, scores and any identifying student data. Show no credit card screen.

## Suggested authentic creative style

- Use the **actual official VisaAtlas logo**, obtainable from the live site's existing brand assets. Never invent a new version or alter its geometry.
- Use **real human photography** obtained with permission or appropriately licensed, or genuine screenshots/screen recordings of the platform. Avoid fake U.S. embassy officers, fabricated interviews, synthetic approval letters, or AI-generated testimonial faces.
- Navy/royal blue, white, ample space; clear CTA. For Instagram feed: 1080×1350. For story/reel cover: 1080×1920. Test text legibility on mobile.
- Avoid claiming VisaAtlas pays SEVIS fees or completes DS-160 applications unless those services are independently validated and operational. Do not claim guaranteed approvals.

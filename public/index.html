      AI mock interviewer

      Ready when you are

      AI practice with a still portrait, not a real consular officer. Microphone only; no camera or video recording.

      The interviewer asks one question at a time and follows up based on the student’s spoken answer.

      Answer naturally after each question. Pause when you finish; the interviewer will respond automatically.

      After the interview

      End your interview to see what went well and what to practise next. Coaching and corrections appear here, after the conversation.

      Daily feedback reports are unlimited, with one saved report per mock interview. Generate one report at a time. If generation fails, you can retry.

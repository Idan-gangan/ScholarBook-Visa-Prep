BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things you may be wondering.

What does VisaAtlas help me do?

VisaAtlas helps you prepare for U.S. F-1 student visa interviews through structured learning, AI tutoring, voice mock interviews, and feedback. You practise explaining your actual study plans and circumstances.

Is this only for student-athletes?

No. VisaAtlas supports high school, undergraduate, master’s, and PhD students. Student-athletes can also include their sporting background in their preparation.

Can I use it after a previous visa denial?

Yes. You can prepare to explain your previous application and any genuine changes in your circumstances. VisaAtlas does not determine eligibility or replace advice from a qualified professional.

Will it give me answers to memorise?

The tutor uses explanations and examples to help you understand questions. Your answers should come from your own plans, experiences, and documents. Practise speaking naturally and truthfully.

What do I need for voice practice?

Use a phone or computer with a microphone, a stable internet connection, and a browser that allows microphone access. A quiet setting and headphones can help. Review the voice notice before starting a session.

What does the readiness feedback mean?

It highlights strengths and areas to work on in your practice interview. It is preparation feedback, not a prediction of a consular decision or a guarantee of visa approval.

Does VisaAtlas book appointments or issue visas?

No. VisaAtlas is an independent learning and preparation platform. Visa applications, appointments, and decisions are handled through the relevant official services.

How do I get started?

Create an account, verify your email, and complete your study profile. Then follow the learning path, practise with the tutor, and take a mock interview when you’re ready.

Privacy Policy

Understand what you share, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Who we are

VisaAtlas is a student interview preparation platform founded by Efe-Sam Agalivie. This notice describes information handled through visaatlasprep.com and our support inbox. For privacy questions or requests, email support@visaatlasprep.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password hash, email verification status, account role, self-declared age group, guardian email and permission record for new students aged 13–17, and the Terms version and acceptance time recorded when you create an account.
  • Study profile: information you provide about your country, phone number, education, chosen school and program, funding, sporting background if relevant, interview location and timing, future plans, and previous applications.
  • Preparation records: saved lesson notes, progress, planning information, mock interview transcripts, and feedback reports.
  • Service and security information: session identifiers, timestamps, usage counters, account changes, and technical information needed to operate and protect the service. Hosting providers may process request and connection logs.
  • Payments: when you choose a plan, we send your account email, selected plan, and payment amount and currency to Paystack to start hosted checkout. We retain transaction references, payment status and verification timestamps, plan details and access records for payment reconciliation, fraud and dispute handling, and support. VisaAtlas does not receive or store your full card number or card security code through the hosted checkout.
  • Secure SEVIS intake (when used): when an adult student completes a private, one-use SEVIS I-901 assistance invitation, we collect their name, SEVIS ID, birth date, school, mailing address, email and phone, the complete Form I-20, passport biodata-page image, and a timestamped processing authorization. We collect these only for the requested SEVIS payment assistance, not for AI mock interviews.
  • Support messages: information you include when you contact us.

Share only information needed for preparation. Do not put passwords, payment card details, passport numbers, or unnecessary sensitive documents in your profile, notes, or conversations.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, provide password recovery, personalise lessons and AI sessions, generate and display feedback, save progress, support students, and prevent misuse, including detecting repeated free-trial registrations. Payment data is used to activate access only after provider verification and to investigate refunds, chargebacks and errors. The authorised owner dashboard also summarises recorded learning updates, mock interview practice and comparable report-score changes to help manage student support. It displays estimated AI voice usage and incomplete cost data where measurements exist; those estimates are not student charges or visa approval predictions. Your preparation information is not a visa application submitted to a government. A separately invited SEVIS intake case is reviewed by VisaAtlas for fee-payment assistance; it does not submit an application or fee by itself.

4. Voice sessions and AI processing

Before each voice session, you see a notice and choose whether to start. Your microphone audio and relevant study profile are sent to OpenAI to provide the conversation and transcription. Tutoring can also use saved lesson notes and relevant feedback. Mock interview transcripts and profile information are sent to OpenAI to generate reports.

VisaAtlas does not store audio or video recordings. It saves mock interview transcripts and feedback. The tutor conversation is not saved by VisaAtlas; notes you choose to save are stored. Stop the session to stop microphone sharing. Browser microphone permission can also be revoked.

OpenAI has its own retention and processing practices; our statement about recordings does not mean that OpenAI retains no data. See OpenAI’s API data controls. Retention can vary with the service and account configuration.

5. Who can access information

You can access your own preparation records. The platform owner and authorised staff can access records for administration and student support; staff access is controlled by student assignments. Owner-only student analytics can show the student’s name and account email alongside saved practice activity, report score trends and usage estimates. This is not a public student directory. SEVIS intake case details and encrypted identity documents are restricted to the authenticated Owner account, with additional password confirmation for review and download; staff and other students do not have access through the intake workflow.

Service providers process information to deliver the platform: OpenAI for AI features, Render for website hosting, the configured database service for saved records, Resend for verification and password recovery emails, Namecheap Private Email for messages sent to our support inbox, and Paystack for checkout and payment processing. Paystack processes payment details under its own privacy and security policies. Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate abuse and protect users.

6. Cookies and browser permissions

VisaAtlas uses an essential sign-in cookie, sb_session, with an eight-hour expiry. It keeps you signed in and is cleared when you sign out. Blocking it prevents authenticated features from working. Microphone permission is required only for voice practice. The current application does not include advertising trackers or an advertising cookie system.

7. Storage, retention, and security

Saved profiles, notes, progress, transcripts, and reports remain in the service so that you can return to your preparation. They are not automatically erased when a session ends. Different records, including payment references and verification records, temporary password reset information and service logs, have different lifetimes. Billing records may be retained as necessary to reconcile purchases, investigate disputes and meet applicable legal duties. Sampled AI cost diagnostics use hashed identifiers rather than stored audio or transcripts and are scheduled for cleanup after 30 days when new diagnostics are recorded; backups may last longer. New student-linked cost samples are removed as part of verified student account deletion. To limit repeat free-trial claims after account deletion, we retain a keyed hash of the trial email for 180 days. This record contains no plain email, profile, transcript, or payment details. Expired hashes are removed when later trial-grant requests run. Aggregate daily trial-start counts are kept for up to 31 UTC dates and cleaned during later trial starts. There is currently no fixed automatic deletion schedule for all student records.

Secure SEVIS intake details and documents are encrypted at the application layer before saving them to the database. Submitted case details and documents are automatically removed from the active intake storage after 30 days during scheduled cleanup, or earlier when the Owner approves a verified deletion request. Private invitation links expire after seven days and work only once. Invitation records may be retained up to 90 days for operational tracking. Database backups and service-provider logs may last longer under their separate retention policies. The intake feature is intended only for adults; please contact support for guardian-assisted processing.

Passwords are stored as hashes, and access controls restrict account and student data. No online service can guarantee complete security. Provider logs and backups may have separate retention periods.

8. Your choices and requests

You can update your study profile, change your password, choose what to include in notes, and stop using voice features. Students can download their profile and saved preparation records as a JSON file from Account security after confirming their current password. Contact support@visaatlasprep.com to request access, correction, a copy of your information, or account and data deletion, including a separate SEVIS intake case. Use your registered email where possible; we may need to verify ownership before acting. Students can delete their account in Account security after confirming their password and typing DELETE. This removes their account and saved preparation records from the active database and ends sign-in access. Finish any voice session or report generation before deleting. Staff and owner accounts should contact support for removal.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, including objection or restriction of processing and the right to complain to a relevant privacy authority. We will assess requests under applicable law and explain any records that must be retained. Removing information from active systems may not immediately remove provider logs or backups.

9. Younger students

VisaAtlas is not intended for children under 13. New students select an age group before registration. Those aged 13–17 need a separate guardian email confirmation and permission declaration before an account is created. We temporarily store the student and guardian email addresses and hashed request tokens; requests expire after 24 hours and expired records are removed during later permission requests or responses. The confirmation records email access and a declaration, not independent verification of age or guardianship. Existing accounts have not been retrospectively verified. Parents and guardians can contact support to withdraw permission or request deletion. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact support so we can investigate and address it. Do not submit another person’s information without appropriate authority.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or email. Questions about this notice can be sent to support@visaatlasprep.com.

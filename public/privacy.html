Privacy Policy

Understand what you share, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Who we are

VisaAtlas is a student interview preparation platform founded by Efe-Sam Agalivie. This notice describes information handled through visaatlasprep.com and our support inbox. For privacy questions or requests, email support@visaatlasprep.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password hash, email verification status, account role, and the Terms version and acceptance time recorded when you create an account.
  • Study profile: information you provide about your country, phone number, education, chosen school and program, funding, sporting background if relevant, interview location and timing, future plans, and previous applications.
  • Preparation records: saved lesson notes, progress, planning information, mock interview transcripts, and feedback reports.
  • Service and security information: session identifiers, timestamps, usage counters, account changes, and technical information needed to operate and protect the service. Hosting providers may process request and connection logs.
  • Support messages: information you include when you contact us.

Share only information needed for preparation. Do not put passwords, payment card details, passport numbers, or unnecessary sensitive documents in your profile, notes, or conversations.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, provide password recovery, personalise lessons and AI sessions, generate and display feedback, save progress, support students, and prevent misuse. Your preparation information is not a visa application submitted to a government.

4. Voice sessions and AI processing

Before each voice session, you see a notice and choose whether to start. Your microphone audio and relevant study profile are sent to OpenAI to provide the conversation and transcription. Tutoring can also use saved lesson notes and relevant feedback. Mock interview transcripts and profile information are sent to OpenAI to generate reports.

VisaAtlas does not store audio or video recordings. It saves mock interview transcripts and feedback. The tutor conversation is not saved by VisaAtlas; notes you choose to save are stored. Stop the session to stop microphone sharing. Browser microphone permission can also be revoked.

OpenAI has its own retention and processing practices; our statement about recordings does not mean that OpenAI retains no data. See OpenAI’s API data controls. Retention can vary with the service and account configuration.

5. Who can access information

You can access your own preparation records. The platform owner and authorised staff can access records for administration and student support; staff access is controlled by student assignments. This is not a public student directory.

Service providers process information to deliver the platform: OpenAI for AI features, Render for website hosting, the configured database service for saved records, Resend for verification and password recovery emails, and Namecheap Private Email for messages sent to our support inbox. Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate abuse and protect users.

6. Cookies and browser permissions

VisaAtlas uses an essential sign-in cookie, sb_session, with an eight-hour expiry. It keeps you signed in and is cleared when you sign out. Blocking it prevents authenticated features from working. Microphone permission is required only for voice practice. The current application does not include advertising trackers or an advertising cookie system.

7. Storage, retention, and security

Saved profiles, notes, progress, transcripts, and reports remain in the service so that you can return to your preparation. They are not automatically erased when a session ends. Different records, such as temporary password reset information and service logs, have different lifetimes. There is currently no fixed automatic deletion schedule for all student records.

Passwords are stored as hashes, and access controls restrict account and student data. No online service can guarantee complete security. Provider logs and backups may have separate retention periods.

8. Your choices and requests

You can update your study profile, change your password, choose what to include in notes, and stop using voice features. Contact support@visaatlasprep.com to request access, correction, a copy of your information, or account and data deletion. Use your registered email where possible; we may need to verify ownership before acting. Students can delete their account in Account security after confirming their password and typing DELETE. This removes their account and saved preparation records from the active database and ends sign-in access. Finish any voice session or report generation before deleting. Staff and owner accounts should contact support for removal.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, including objection or restriction of processing and the right to complain to a relevant privacy authority. We will assess requests under applicable law and explain any records that must be retained. Removing information from active systems may not immediately remove provider logs or backups.

9. Younger students

VisaAtlas is not intended for children under 13. Students under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian’s permission. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact support so we can investigate and address it. Do not submit another person’s information without appropriate authority.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or email. Questions about this notice can be sent to support@visaatlasprep.com.
